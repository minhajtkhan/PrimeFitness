# 🏋️ Prime Fitness Club

**Stay Fit. Stay Strong. Stay Prime.**

A modern, responsive multi-page website for **Prime Fitness Club**, a premium fitness and wellness center established in 1982. The site presents the club's training programs, facilities, memberships, trainers, and wellness services in a sleek, glass-style design.

**Live Demo:** https://minhajtkhan.github.io/PrimeFitness/

---

## About the Project

Prime Fitness Club is a static front-end website that showcases a full-service gym: from cycling studios and an Olympic-size pool to group classes, a luxury spa, and flexible membership plans. It is designed to give visitors a clear picture of everything the club offers and make it easy to get in touch.

---

## Pages

| Page | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Home** | Hero with background video, quick navigation, key stats, core programs, trainers, testimonials, and blog highlights |
| **Cycling** | Indoor cycling studio and sessions |
| **Swimming** | Aquatic centre and swim programs |
| **Sports & Fitness** | Training floor, machines, and functional training zones |
| **Group Exercise** | Weekly classes such as Yoga, Pilates, Zumba, BodyPump, HIIT, and Boxing |
| **Membership** | Flexible membership plans |
| **Salon & Spa** | Massage, facials, sauna, steam room, and beauty salon |
| **Gallery** | Photos of the club and facilities |
| **Equipment List** | Available gym equipment |
| **About Us** | The club's story and team |
| **FAQ** | Answers to common questions |
| **Enquiry** | Contact and guest pass enquiry form |

## Highlights

- 🎥 **Full-screen hero** with looping background video
- 🧊 **Glassmorphism design** with a sticky glass navbar and decorative gradient orbs
- 🧭 **Quick-navigation cards** to jump straight to each service
- 📊 **Stats banner**: 40+ years of heritage, 15K+ members, 100+ workstations, 98% retention
- 🃏 **Program cards** with hover animations
- 👨‍🏫 **Trainer profiles** with certifications and bios
- ⭐ **Member testimonials** with star ratings
- 📰 **Wellness blog section** with article previews
- 📣 **Call-to-action bands** for memberships and enquiries
- ✨ **Scroll-reveal animations** and a scroll indicator
- 📱 **Fully responsive** on desktop, tablet, and mobile
