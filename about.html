OUR HISTORY

FORTY YEARS OF HERITAGE

Prime Fitness Club was founded in 1982 as a premier powerlifting and conditioning basement gym. Over the decades, we have continuously modernized, integrating biomechanical smart machines, salt-water purification pools, and dedicated spa suites.

Today, we stand as the intersection of high-fidelity luxury wellness and peak physical performance, serving thousands of athletes across the region.
